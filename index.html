Our Vision

We envision a Scotland in which cultural diversity is actively valued, and where shared musical experiences contribute to personal wellbeing, social connection and a strong sense of collective belonging.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote cultural diversity, personal wellbeing and social interconnectedness by bringing world music ensembles and audiences together in inclusive, accessible shared spaces. Through live music and cultural exchange, we aim to support emotional wellbeing, reduce social isolation and foster understanding, mutual respect and connection across communities.

We work to support artists, engage audiences and create opportunities for meaningful participation with diverse musical traditions, recognising music as a powerful tool for individual wellbeing and for strengthening social bonds.

Core Values

  • Diversity
  • Community
  • Participation
  • Fair Pay

Who We Benefit

Primary beneficiaries

  • Audiences and community participants of all ages who benefit from increased access to live music, cultural exchange and shared creative experiences that support personal wellbeing and reduce social isolation.
  • People from diverse cultural, ethnic and migratory backgrounds, particularly those who may experience marginalisation or limited representation within mainstream cultural spaces.
  • Local communities, including intergenerational groups, who benefit from opportunities to gather, connect and build social cohesion through shared musical activity.

Secondary beneficiaries

  • World music ensembles and musicians, who benefit from fair paid performance opportunities, professional support and increased visibility for their work.
  • Emerging and underrepresented artists, who gain access to platforms that support sustainable practice, collaboration and audience development.

What We Offer

  • Online Information
  • Musician Residencies
  • Social Events
  • Record Label and Album Reviews
